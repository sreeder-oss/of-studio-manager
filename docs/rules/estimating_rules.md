# Organic Finishes LLC — Estimating & Pricing Rules (2026–2027 Master)

## 1. Core Philosophy: Turnkey Craftsman Pricing (No Bloat)
- All pricing is transparent and direct: flat turnkey rates per square foot, or clear hourly/day rates plus materials at landed cost.
- **Profit is built directly into the labor rate** — no hidden overhead multipliers or surprise markups.
- **Materials pass through at actual landed cost** (product invoice + freight/shipping, 0% markup).
- **Containment & Consumables (Day-Rate Jobs):** Flat \$75 – \$150 allowance per job (masking poly, FrogTape, Festool HEPA bags & abrasives, foam rollers, buckets).
- Standard waste factor on materials: **12%**.
- **Regional Tiering:**
  - **Tier 1 (Core Local — OK, AR, KS, MO):** Tulsa, OKC, NW Arkansas, Wichita, Springfield — bid the lower-to-mid band to win work cleanly while protecting daily yield.
  - **Tier 2 (Major Regional Metros — DFW, Austin, Houston, Kansas City):** Higher prevailing market rates — bid the mid-to-high band plus Tier-2 travel.
