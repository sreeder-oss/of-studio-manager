# Organic Finishes LLC — Estimating & Pricing Rules (2026 Master)

## 1. Core Philosophy: Turnkey Craftsman Pricing (No Bloat)
- All pricing is transparent and direct: flat labor rates per square foot or per day.
- **Profit is built directly into the labor rate.**
- **Materials pass through at cost** (no artificial markups or hidden 38% formula multipliers).
- Standard waste factor on materials: 12%.
