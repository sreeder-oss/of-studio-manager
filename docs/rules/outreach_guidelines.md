# Organic Finishes LLC — Outreach & Client Pipeline Guidelines

## 1. Directory Structure: Clients vs. Potential Clients

### Clients (Active Contracting)
- **Who they are:** Homeowners, GCs, commercial clients, or designers for whom Organic Finishes is preparing estimates, executing active projects, or billing invoices.
- **App Actions:** Create Estimate, Convert to Invoice, Track Project Expenses/Receipts, Upload Photos.

### Potential Clients (Email Outreach & Spec Sharing)
- **Who they are:** Regional commercial General Contractors, high-end residential builders, interior design studios, architects, and retail developers across Oklahoma, Texas, Arkansas, Kansas, and Missouri.
- **Purpose:** Emailing technical information, CSI Division 09 system specs, Microcement rules, dust extraction capabilities, and design application guides.
- **Promotion to Client:** When a Potential Client responds with architectural drawings, plans, or asks for a proposal, **promote them with 1 click to Client** and generate the estimate.

## 3. Core Outreach Pitches by Lead Category

### Pitch 1: Waterproof Microcement Showers & Wet Rooms (Zero Grout)
- **Target:** Luxury interior designers, custom home builders, boutique hospitality.
- **Key Talking Point:** Eliminating porous grout lines and maintenance callbacks through a continuous, hand-troweled multi-layer system with liquid waterproofing and embedded fiberglass mesh.

### Pitch 2: Commercial Off-Hours / HEPA Dust Extraction
- **Target:** Commercial GCs, retail remodelers, restaurant groups.
- **Key Talking Point:** Festool HEPA dust extraction allows work in occupied or sensitive environments; overnight / off-hours scheduling prevents business interruption.

### Pitch 3: Demolition-Free Surface Renovations
- **Target:** Commercial renovations, high-end kitchen/bath remodels.
- **Key Talking Point:** Microcement bonds tenaciously over sound tile, concrete, and drywall, bypassing days of noisy demolition, landfill waste, and debris hauling.

### Pitch 4: Venetian & Artisan Lime Plasters
- **Target:** High-end residential architects, interior designers.
- **Key Talking Point:** Authentic mineral Marmorino and Tadelakt with integral earth pigments and custom sample boards matching architectural palettes.
